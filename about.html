02 / About Kaysons

A focused engineering company for hazardous-area equipment.

Established in 1996, Kaysons Techno Equipments Private Limited develops explosion-protected electrical equipment for demanding industrial applications.

Engineering and manufacturing operations are based in Vitthal Udyognagar, Gujarat, India.
Our foundation
Kaysons combines application understanding with manufacturing discipline to build electrical equipment for hazardous areas. The work begins with the requirement—not with a generic catalogue selection.

Our product scope covers Ex d, Ex e and Ex t concepts for Group I, IIA, IIB and IIC applications, subject to the relevant product approval and certificate.

We support projects across oil & gas, refineries, chemical and petrochemical facilities, mining, utilities, process industries and manufacturing.
