About Kaysons

Hazardous-area engineering since 1996.

Kaysons Techno Equipments Private Limited designs and manufactures electrical equipment for demanding industrial environments.

Company profile

Specialisation built on
manufacturing experience.

Established on 13 February 1996, Kaysons serves industrial requirements for explosion-protected electrical equipment from its registered office and works in Vitthal Udyognagar, Gujarat.

Our operations bring together application engineering, design, machining, assembly, inspection, testing and quality control. Casting and fabrication form part of the coordinated manufacturing flow.

The portfolio spans lighting, local control, power connection, panels, field termination, instrument protection and installation accessories.
