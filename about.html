02 / About us

A focused engineering company for hazardous-area electrical equipment.

Established on 13 February 1996, Kaysons Techno Equipments Private Limited serves industrial requirements for explosion-protected electrical equipment from its registered office and works in Vitthal Udyognagar, Gujarat.

Focused on clear technical dialogue, application alignment and dependable project coordination.
Company profile
“Protection begins with understanding the environment, the application and the consequence of failure.”

Since 1996, Kaysons has concentrated on one demanding field: electrical equipment intended for hazardous industrial locations.

Our Vitthal Udyognagar manufacturing operations bring together application engineering, design, machining, assembly, inspection, testing and quality control. Casting and fabrication form part of the coordinated manufacturing flow for project-specific equipment.

The portfolio spans lighting, local control, power connection, panels, field termination, instrument protection and installation accessories for oil and gas, refineries, petrochemicals, chemicals, mining, power and process industries.
