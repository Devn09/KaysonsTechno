Product guidance

A few useful
answers.

General guidance for initial selection. Confirm the selected model’s certificate, technical documentation and project requirements.

Ask our team
01

What types of explosion-proof equipment does Kaysons manufacture?

Kaysons manufactures product families including lighting fittings, control stations and motor starters, plugs and sockets, control and instrument panels, junction boxes, instrument enclosures, limit switches, temperature-sensor heads, cable glands, conduit accessories, Ex components and increased-safety enclosures.

02

Which hazardous-area protection concepts are covered?

The Kaysons portfolio includes equipment developed around Ex d, Ex e and Ex t protection concepts. The applicable protection concept must be confirmed from the certificate and technical documentation for the selected product and model.

03

Which gas groups can Kaysons equipment cover?

The portfolio includes provisions for Group I mining applications and industrial gas groups IIA, IIB and IIC. Exact gas-group suitability is product- and certificate-specific and must be verified during selection.

04

Which hazardous-area zones are supported?

Products may be available for gas hazardous areas in Zones 1 and 2 and dust hazardous areas in Zones 21 and 22, depending on the equipment type, protection concept and certificate.

05

Are Kaysons products available with IECEx, ATEX and PESO certification?

Kaysons maintains a portfolio supported by certifications and approvals including IECEx, ATEX and PESO. Certification is specific to the product, model, construction and application, so the relevant certificate should be checked before specification.

06

Can Kaysons support project-specific equipment and documentation?

Yes. Kaysons can review project requirements and propose suitable equipment or project-specific configurations within the scope of applicable standards and certifications. Product photographs, certificates, technical datasheets and catalogues can be provided as they become available for the selected range.
